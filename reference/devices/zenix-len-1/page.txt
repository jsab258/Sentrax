What is ZENIX LEN-1
ZENIX is a cutting-edge family of IoT locators and gateways built to power smart buildings, healthcare, and industrial applications.
The ZENIX LEN-1 is a BLE Indoor Scanner/Locator designed to help businesses gain real-time visibility of their assets and operations. With built-in PoE and Wi-Fi connectivity, it seamlessly integrates into existing environments and scans BLE beacons to deliver accurate, real-time location insights. The LEN-1 enables better tracking, improved safety, and enhanced operational efficiency, without requiring complex installation or maintenance.
It supports advanced capabilities such as Proximity BLE tag tracking , People Positioning , and BLE Tag Health Monitoring .
