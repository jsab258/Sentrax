What is PINIX TOW-5?
The PINIX TOW-5 is a high-end BLE 5.x AoA asset-tracking tag designed for enterprise asset management. It combines motion, temperature, humidity, and pressure sensors with a user button, LED, and IP65-rated rugged enclosure, providing a reliable all-in-one solution for industrial, logistics, healthcare, and smart-building deployments. Its replaceable lithium battery offers up to 4 years of low-maintenance operation with precise AoA-based positioning.
Its core capabilities include precision asset tracking, motion detection, environmental sensing, and reliable indoor/outdoor BLE positioning.
Key Features &#038; Benefits
Protection
IP65-rated rugged enclosure, resistant to dust and low-pressure water jets.
Motion Detection
Embedded 3-axis accelerometer for accurate motion tracking.
Battery Life
Replaceable lithium battery with up to 4 years of low-maintenance operation.
Environmental Sensing
Monitors temperature, humidity, and pressure for asset safety.
How It Works?
The PINIX TOW-5 is a BLE 5.x AoA asset-tracking tag designed for real-time location, motion, and environmental monitoring in industrial, logistics, healthcare, and smart-building environments.
