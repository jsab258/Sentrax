What is ZENIX LEF-3
The ZENIX LEF-3 is a durable, outdoor BLE RSSI locator and gateway designed for harsh environments where you need reliable BLE-based proximity tracking. Built with a rugged IP67 enclosure, GPS, LTE connectivity, and external antennas, it scans nearby BLE beacons/tags and forwards signal strength data (RSSI) to your RTLS infrastructure. With its embedded processor running Sentrax firmware and flexible network interfaces (PoE, Wi-Fi, LTE), the LEF-3 is ideal for outdoor RTLS use cases like yard tracking, people safety, and large outdoor assets.
It supports advanced capabilities such as Proximity BLE Tag Tracking , People Tracking , BLE Tags Healthcheck monitoring
Key Features &#038; Benefits
🅱️
Outdoor RSSI-based BLE Scanning
Uses BLE 5.1 to scan for tag or beacon signals, analyzes received signal strength (RSSI) to infer proximity.
📍
Long-Range Coverage
External antennas provide extended BLE scan range (10–50 m, depending on environment).
🌡️
Compact, Outdoor Mounting
Comes with a wall or pole mount system for flexible outdoor installation.
🎯
Remote Tag Configuration &#038; Updates
Supports remote configuration and firmware updates of connected tags.
