Whats is ZENIX LEN-2
ZENIX LEN-2 is an advanced Bluetooth® Low Energy (BLE) RSSI gateway designed for Smart Buildings, Healthcare, and Industrial Environments. It provides reliable real-time beacon detection, asset &#038; person proximity location visibility, and environmental sensor data collection within its coverage area.
Equipped with PoE and Wi-Fi connectivity, an external antenna for extended indoor coverage, and Sentrax&#8217;s high-performance BLE scanning firmware, the LEN-2 delivers continuous location and sensor data. Its compact design and easy wall or ceiling mounting enables fast deployment across a wide range of indoor applications.
Key Features &#038; Benefits
🎯
3-5m Accuracy
Proximity real-time indoor positioning with meter-level tracking accuracy.
🔗
PoE &#038; Wi-Fi Connectivity
Flexible deployment with Power over Ethernet and Wi-Fi connectivity.

Smart BLE Filtering
Advanced beacon filtering for improved scanning performance and reduced noise.

Configure devices, update firmware, and manage settings remotely.
How It Works?
The ZENIX BLE LEN-2 is a BLE Indoor Scanner gateway that enables real-time tracking and presence detection across industries, healthcare, and smart buildings, offering reliable performance and easy installation.
