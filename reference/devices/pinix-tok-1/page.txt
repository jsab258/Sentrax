What is PINIX TOK-1?
The PINIX TOK-1 is a Smart Badge BLE 5.x AoA tag engineered for person tracking, indoor localization, and temperature monitoring. It integrates a motion sensor, internal temperature sensor, user button, LED indicators, and an IP65-rated rugged enclosure, offering a versatile solution for smart-building, workplace safety, and indoor navigation applications. Its replaceable CR2032 button cell ensures low-maintenance operation with up to 6 months of battery life, supporting reliable AoA-based positioning.
Its core capabilities include precision asset tracking, precision tracking, motion detection, temperature monitoring, and a panic/SOS button.
Key Features &#038; Benefits
Protection
IP65-rated ABS enclosure, resistant to dust and low-pressure water jets.
Battery Life
Replaceable 225mAh CR2032 battery with 4–6 months of operation depending on advertisement rate.
Temperature Monitoring
Built-in sensor for monitoring environmental conditions.
User Interaction
Single interactable button and tri-color LEDs (Blue, Red, Green) for notifications.
How It Works?
The PINIX TOK-1 is a BLE 5.x AoA smart badge designed for real-time people tracking, motion sensing, and temperature monitoring in workplaces, smart buildings, and indoor navigation environments.
