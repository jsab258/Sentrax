What is PINIX TOW-1?
The PINIX TOW-1 is a hybrid BLE RSSI &#038; AoA asset-tracking tag engineered for high-performance, location-based applications. By combining wide-area proximity detection with optional sub-meter AoA accuracy, it delivers reliable and precise tracking across demanding industrial and commercial environments.
Powered by BLE 5.x with AoA , the PINIX TOW-1 integrates an LED indicator , a multifunction button , a replaceable 1000 mAh lithium battery , and IP65-rated protection , ensuring a strong balance of durability, efficiency, and long-term value.
Its core capabilities include asset tracking , motion detection , and indoor/outdoor positioning , making it a versatile and dependable choice for modern RTLS deployments.
Key Features &#038; Benefits
Battery Life
Features Extended battery life and a replaceable battery for long-term, low-maintenance operation.
Motion Detection
Embedded 3-Axis Accelerometer for motion detection
Protection
IP65-Rated enclosure, offering high resistance to dust ingress and low-pressure water jets
Build &#038; Design
Features robust ABS housing with an ultra-lightweight 45 g design for effortless asset mounting.
