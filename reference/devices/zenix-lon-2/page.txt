What is ZENIX LON-2
The ZENIX LON-2 is a high-precision indoor BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) locator and gateway, built for advanced Real-Time Location Systems (RTLS) in smart buildings, healthcare, industrial, and asset-tracking environments. Leveraging Angle of Arrival (AoA) technology, it delivers sub-meter accuracy in positioning nearby tags. With its embedded processor running Sentrax’s firmware and flexible connectivity via PoE and optional Wi-Fi, the LON-2 reliably captures real-time angle and sensor data from Beacon/Tag devices. Its durable design supports easy mounting on ceilings or walls, forming the cornerstone of a highly accurate location-tracking infrastructure.
It supports advanced capabilities such as Sub-Meter Level BLE Tag Tracking , Asset Tracking , People Tracking , BLE Tags Health Check Monitoring
Key Features &#038; Benefits
🅱️
Sub-Meter Location Accuracy
Delivers highly accurate indoor positioning, typically within 0.2–0.5 meters.
📍
Beacon / Tag PDU Extraction
Captures and decodes BLE PDU data for telemetry, identification, and sensor insights.
⚙️
Remote Tag Configuration &#038; Updates
Enables remote setup and firmware updates of connected tags for easier device management.
🎯
AoA Filtering Configuration
Supports adjustable moving-average filtering to stabilize and refine angle measurements.
How It Works?
The ZENIX LON-2 is an AoA-enabled Indoor Locator Gateway that delivers sub-meter real-time positioning for assets and personnel, providing high-precision tracking for healthcare, smart buildings, industrial environments, and advanced RTLS deployments.

Smart Buildings
Provides accurate AoA-based indoor positioning for high-density areas, BLE-based presence detection for offices and meeting rooms, and enhances building automation with real-time occupancy and movement insights.
