Product Overview
How it Works?
Why Choose NODIX CEN-1? Room-Level Certainty. Half the Infrastructure.
The NODIX CEN-1 is a bi-directional BLE anchor relay that delivers reliable room-level visibility in complex indoor environments. In large facilities, single scanners often fail to cover all areas, creating blind spots that disrupt asset tracking, workflows, and operational control. Optimized for high-density deployments, it enables precise localization without requiring one gateway per room. It can also be placed in passageways and gates to extend coverage and ensure seamless tracking across zones.With high operational reliability, simple installation, and a replaceable high-capacity battery, the NODIX CEN-1 significantly reduces deployment complexity and maintenance overhead. By minimizing the need for multiple RSSI gateways, it provides a cost-efficient and scalable RTLS architecture, making large-scale implementations both practical and sustainable. Seamless integration with existing systems and intuitive configuration tools allows for fast deployment and effortless system expansion. The Hybrid Advantage
To protect your infrastructure investment and optimize ROI, the Sentrax ecosystem utilizes a Hybrid Deployment Model. We distinguish between high-capacity tracking and room-level presence to give you the most efficient footprint possible: ZENIX LEN Gateways (The Brains):
Deployed in hallways and open wards, these high-bandwidth powerhouses manage the data backbone, higher number of tag detection with lowest latency, provide real-time movement tracking, and act as the central gateways for your entire facility.
NODIX CEN-1 BiLink Anchors (The Eyes):
Deployed inside private rooms, exam bays, or closets to provide absolute certainty of presence. The NODIX &#8220;sees&#8221; local tags and wirelessly &#8220;whispers&#8221; data back to the ZENIX gateway.
Instead of requiring installation of gateway and an IP-addressable scanner in every room, the NODIX CEN-1 acts as a wireless extension for your ZENIX infrastructure: Local Scan:
The NODIX CEN-1 anchor scans its immediate room for any BLE tags (equipment, patients, or staff).
Wireless Bypass:
It bundles the tag data with its own unique Room ID and wirelessly relays it to the nearest ZENIX Hallway Scanner.
Instant Identification:
The Sentrax positioning engine identifies exactly which room the tag is in, providing 100% room-level accuracy while reducing total infrastructure requirements by up to 60%.
Zero-Cabling Deployment: No need for additional installation in in every room. Few central scanners can manage multiple NODIX anchors.
No IT Complexity: Reach deep into private rooms and &#8220;blind spots&#8221; without adding new IP addresses or network overhead.
Presence-Based Certainty: Eliminates &#8220;signal bleed&#8221; through walls. If the NODIX in Room 101 sees a tag, that tag is in Room 101—period.
Rapid Scalability: Deploy room-level tracking in minutes, not days, with a zero-cable mounting process.
Optimized ROI: Achieve high-granularity tracking at a hardware cost-per-room that is up to 60% lower than traditional gateway-only setups.

How BiLink Solves RTLS Challenges?
Problem
In large facilities with multiple rooms of varying sizes and layouts, a single scanner cannot effectively cover the entire area. This results in blind spots where movement of assets, staff, or equipment goes undetected, leading to asset loss, delayed response times, workflow inefficiencies, and lack of true facility-wide real-time visibility.
Attempting to eliminate these blind spots by deploying scanners in every room is often not practical, as it significantly increases infrastructure complexity, cabling requirements, maintenance effort, and overall deployment cost.
Solution
We have developed NODIX CEN-1 to eliminate blind spots and enable seamless room-level visibility across complex indoor environments. It ensures consistent tracking even in facilities with multiple rooms of varying sizes and layouts, without the need for a scanner in every room.
By intelligently extending coverage across spaces, it delivers continuous, real-time visibility throughout the facility while keeping infrastructure minimal and cost-efficient.
