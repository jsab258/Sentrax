What is PINIX TOB-1?
The PINIX TOB-1 is a Bluetooth® Low Energy 5.x wearable beacon tag designed for accurate indoor positioning, personnel safety, vulnerable tracking, and emergency response across healthcare, industrial, enterprise, and high-security environments. Powered by the Nordic nRF52 chipset, it supports BLE AoA, multiple Bluetooth advertising protocols, an integrated SOS emergency button, an IP67-rated enclosure, a replaceable CR2032 battery, and a built-in accelerometer for reliable real-time people location monitoring.
Key Features &#038; Benefits
BLE AoA Positioning
Enables precise indoor positioning for reliable real-time location monitoring across complex indoor environments.
Integrated SoS Button
Provides immediate emergency assistance by allowing users to trigger instant distress alerts during critical situations.
CR2032 Battery Replaceable
Equipped with a replaceable 220mAh coin-cell battery for continuous operation and easy battery replacement.
IP67 Rugged Wearable Design
Combines an IP67-rated enclosure and integrated accelerometer for dependable performance in demanding working environments.
How It Works?
The PINIX TOB-1 Wearable tag broadcasts BLE signals to Sentrax AoA scanners, which process them through the RTLS platform to deliver precise indoor person positioning and emergency response.

Applications &#038; Use Cases
The PINIX TOB-1 is designed for applications requiring accurate indoor people positioning, emergency response, and personnel safety. Its BLE AoA technology, integrated SoS button, and wearable design make it ideal for real-time location monitoring across healthcare, industrial, enterprise, and high-security environments.
Indoor Localization
Enable precise real-time indoor person positioning to improve operational visibility, workforce coordination, and location-based services across complex facilities.
Emergency Rescue &#038; Personnel Safety
Enable workers to instantly trigger emergency alerts using the integrated SoS button, helping teams respond faster during critical incidents.
Location-Based Services
Support location-aware applications by providing accurate positioning data for navigation, monitoring, and operational decision-making within indoor environments.
Healthcare Safety
Enable real-time positioning of patients, vulnerable individuals, and mother–child pairs in healthcare environments, with an integrated SOS button for instant emergency alerts and improved safety response.
Restricted Zone Access &#038; Geofencing
Create virtual zones and instantly detect when personnel enter or exit restricted areas. Improve workplace safety, security compliance, and real-time monitoring across industrial plants, laboratories, and high-security facilities.
Attendance &#038; Workforce Analytics
Automatically record person presence, working hours, and movement patterns to improve workforce utilization, optimize operations, and simplify attendance management.
