Experience Precise Location Intelligence with BLE AoA RTLS
Sentrax&#8217;s BLE AoA-based RTLS system redefines location tracking accuracy using Angle of Arrival (AoA)
technology. By capturing the direction of incoming Bluetooth signals with multi-antenna locators, the
system delivers sub-meter positional accuracy across complex environments.
Whether you’re tracking critical equipment, optimizing workflows, or enhancing safety, Sentrax AoA RTLS
enables precise, real-time spatial awareness with industry-leading precision.

Scalable. Accurate. Purpose-Built.
Sentrax is providing cutting-edge location-tracking solutions that utilize Angle-of-Arrival (AoA)
technology, which allows for real-time tracking with sub-meter accuracy. By combining this
technology with proprietary algorithms, Sentrax offers clients highly accurate and reliable
location-tracking system.
From fast-paced hospitals to complex logistics centers, Sentrax BLE AoA RTLS delivers
enterprise-grade precision and reliability. Multi-antenna locators measure signal angles in realtime, while Solix Suite intelligently computes positions and offers intuitive visualization.
This is not just tracking—it&#8217;s location intelligence.
