Indoor &#038; Outdoor Real-Time Tracking Made Simple
Improve operations with Sentrax&#8217;s Hybrid RTLS and environmental sensing system. By combining BLE Angle-of-Arrival (AoA) precision with RSSI-based coverage, the system delivers real-time tracking and environmental insights across diverse spaces—helping you increase efficiency, enhance safety, and reduce operational costs.
Track inventory movement, monitor personnel and assets, and keep an eye on environmental conditions with ease.
Key Benefits
📍
Precision Meets Coverage
Sub-meter AoA where needed + room-level RSSI coverage across large spaces.
🌿

Monitor temperature, humidity, and air quality alongside location data.

Operational Efficiency
Reduce search times, automate workflows, and avoid costly delays.

🧱

Scale tracking across facilities while keeping hardware and installation costs optimized.

IoT &#038; API Ready
Hybrid RTLS leverages BLE’s ubiquity and integrates effortlessly via REST APIs and Solix middleware.
The Power of Hybrid RTLS
From the Sentrax GmbH perspective, a Hybrid RTLS is a versatile, future-ready solution for industries requiring both high precision and wide coverage. By integrating AoA for sub-meter accuracy in critical zones with RSSI for large-area monitoring.
Indoor Applications: Hospitals, smart buildings and warehouses.
Outdoor Extensions: Loading bays, campus areas and airside airport zones.
End-to-End Visibility: Ensuring that no asset and tool, or team member is out of sight.
This hybrid approach simplifies complex operations, supports regulatory compliance, and maximizes ROI by using the right technology in the right zone.

How Sentrax Hybrid RTLS Works
A comprehensive real-time location system combining AoA and RSSI technologies for precise indoor positioning
1
Define Zones
Configure spaces as either AoA Zones (requiring high accuracy) or RSSI Zones (requiring basic presence tracking).
AoA &#038; RSSI Zones
2
Deploy Devices
Install Zenix AoA Locators in precision-critical areas and RSSI BLE Scanners in larger coverage zones.
ZENIX BLE AoA &#038; RSSI Scanners
3
Hybrid Tag Detection
BLE tags (e.g., PINIX TOW-1, TOP-5) automatically transmit signals detectable by both locator types.
PINIX AoA &#038; RSSI Tags
4
Solix Positioning Engine
The Solix Engine calculates real-time positions using AoA and RSSI, tailored to your zone settings.
Real-Time Processing &#038; Easy Integration
5
Live Visualization
Solix Suite enables precise hybrid tag tracking with AoA and RSSI, plus easy geofences and smart alerts.
