Experience Real-Time Proximity Tracking System
Sentrax’s BLE RSSI-based RTLS solution is a cutting-edge technology designed to elevate operational efficiency through intelligent location tracking. Powered by Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), it provides real-time proximity monitoring of assets, persons, and equipment within any defined space.
Whether it&#8217;s managing workflows, enhancing safety, or streamlining processes, this system provides the spatial awareness needed to make smarter, faster decisions.
