Manufacturing
How RTLS works in Manufacturing?
Sentrax RTLS transforms manufacturing, warehousing, and logistics operations with real-time, sub-meter, and room-level visibility. Track assets, monitor personnel, automate workflows, and gain the location intelligence needed to improve productivity and accelerate decision-making.
Key Benefits:
Instantly locate tools, pallets, forklifts, equipment, and inventory.
Prevent bottlenecks with automated geofencing and real-time alerts.
Improve workforce productivity through live staff positioning and zone-based monitoring.
Optimize operations with actionable, real-time location insights.
Goods Localization
The Challenge
Warehouse teams spend significant time searching for misplaced pallets, containers and inventory. Barcode and RFID processes rely on manual scans, creating visibility gaps when movements are not recorded. This leads to inaccurate inventory data, inefficient picking routes, delayed order fulfilment, higher labour costs and difficulty maintaining effective warehouse operations.
How RTLS Solves This?
Sentrax RTLS continuously tracks tagged goods, pallets and containers and displays their locations on live facility maps. Operators can instantly locate inventory, monitor movements and evaluate storage utilization. Movement history and zone-based data provide complete traceability across receiving, storage and shipping.
Business Impacts
Instantly locate pallets, goods, and containers
Reduce search time
Increase inventory accuracy and traceability
Reduce inventory shrinkage and loss
Optimize warehouse storage utilization
Reduce operational costs and manual processes

Work-in-Progress (WIP) Tracking
The Challenge
Manufacturers often lack real-time visibility of assemblies, components, and production orders moving through the production line. Manual and paper-based updates make WIP status difficult to determine, causing bottlenecks, workflow inefficiencies, and production delays. Managers also struggle to balance production lines and maintain schedules.
How Sentrax RTLS Solves This
Sentrax RTLS automatically tracks production orders, assemblies and carriers as they move between workstations. High-precision indoor positioning and live dashboards show each WIP asset’s current location and status, helping manufacturers identify delays, improve production flow and make faster operational decisions.

Staff Duress & Safety Monitoring
The Challenge
Responding quickly to emergencies in large factories and warehouses requires immediate visibility of personnel. Manual reporting, radio communication and physical headcounts can delay the location of workers, evacuation monitoring and coordination of an effective emergency response.
How Sentrax RTLS Solves This
Wearable staff and duress badges provide real-time personnel visibility and allow workers to trigger panic alerts. SOLIX Live dashboards show precise locations, emergency alerts, evacuation progress and muster-point attendance, helping safety teams locate distressed workers, coordinate responses and protect personnel more effectively.
Business Impacts
Locate distressed or injured workers in seconds
Accelerate emergency response times
Improve worker safety and security
Automate evacuation and muster point monitoring
Reduce manual headcounts and safety checks
Support workplace safety and compliance initiatives
Increase situational awareness during emergencies

Real-Time Access Control & Zone Monitoring
The Challenge
Organizations need to prevent unauthorized access to restricted, hazardous and sensitive areas while maintaining real-time visibility of personnel inside each zone. Traditional access control systems record door entry and exit but cannot track movement after entry, creating blind spots around occupancy, dwell time and zone violations. This increases security risks and makes safety and compliance monitoring more difficult.
How Sentrax RTLS Solves This
Sentrax RTLS uses wearable personnel and visitor badges to monitor zone occupancy in real time. The system detects entry into designated areas, alerts teams to unauthorized access or zone violations, and shows who is present and how long they have remained. Historical access records support investigations, compliance reporting and improved worker safety

Climate Monitoring for Warehouses
The Challenge
Pharmaceutical warehouses, cold storage facilities, and other temperature-sensitive environments must maintain strict temperature and humidity conditions to protect product quality and comply with regulatory requirements.
How Sentrax RTLS Solves This
Sentrax provides continuous temperature and humidity monitoring at both warehouse and pallet level. Environmental conditions are monitored across rooms, storage zones and individual pallets containing sensitive goods. Real-time alerts notify teams when configured thresholds are exceeded, enabling faster corrective action, reducing product exposure and supporting complete environmental traceability.
Business Impacts
Monitor temperature and humidity in real time
Receive instant alerts for environmental excursions
Protect pharmaceuticals and temperature-sensitive products
Reduce product spoilage and inventory losses
